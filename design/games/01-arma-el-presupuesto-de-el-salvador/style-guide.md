# Game 1 Visual Foundation

Reference source: `sources/reference/game-1-reference.png`.

## Direction

Use a friendly civic education style: clean, modern, optimistic, and game-like without feeling like a marketing page. The interface should feel like a guided activity for students and citizens.

## Palette

- Deep blue: `#0D47A1` for titles, primary buttons, section tabs, and the player's budget.
- Teal: `#0097A7` for accents, secondary emphasis, and comparison highlights.
- Green: `#43A047` for positive reserve/surplus states.
- Sky blue: `#4FC3F7` for soft illustration fills and background depth.
- Yellow: `#FFC107` for coins, warnings, and reward moments.
- Coral: `#FF7043` for health/social contrast.
- Purple: `#7E57C2` for institutional/social categories.
- Neutral gray: `#607D8B` for official/real budget comparison.
- Light gray: `#ECEFF1` for tracks and inactive surfaces.
- Page background: `#F7FAFC`.

## UI Rules

- Use white cards with pale blue borders and subtle shadows.
- Use deep-blue capsule headers for major modules.
- Primary actions are deep blue with white text; secondary actions are white with deep-blue border/text.
- Sector rows use simple icons, a colored accent, and a clear allocation control.
- Keep the assignment screen focused on the player's choices; real budget values appear on the comparison screen.
- Use small friendly civic illustrations as support art: government building, flag, coins, vault, mountains/city skyline, and guide character motifs.

## Implementation Notes

- The current Phaser prototype should use this palette and component rhythm as its baseline.
- Future sprite/UI work should extend this board instead of introducing a new visual language.
